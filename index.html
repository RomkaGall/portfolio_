About

Shopify development with a front-end focus

I build and refine Shopify storefronts — custom themes, Online Store 2.0 migrations, theme customization, and third-party integrations. Small tasks, advanced Liquid work, and monthly support are all part of the practice.

Projects span fashion, beauty, food, sports, jewellery, electronics, and more — for Ukrainian stores and international brands that need reliable, conversion-ready front ends.
